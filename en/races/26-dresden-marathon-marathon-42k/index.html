The course

About the course

26. DRESDEN-MARATHON – Marathon is a road race in Dresden, Germany, over 42.3 km. The course climbs 198 m and descends 199 m in total, 4.7 m per km, which makes it flat. The highest point is 144 m above sea level and the lowest 104 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 1.0: 32 m of ascent over 5.5 km at 0.6 % on average, up to 144 m.

The organizer lists 7 aid stations, the first at km 9.0 and the last at km 34.9. There are 2 cut-off times along the course.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.

Frequently asked questions

When is 26. DRESDEN-MARATHON – Marathon 42.3 km?

The race took place on Sunday, 25 October 2026. The date of the next edition is not set yet.

How long is 26. DRESDEN-MARATHON – Marathon 42.3 km and how much climbing is there?

The race is 42.3 km with 198 m of climbing and 199 m of descent. The highest point is 144 m above sea level.

What is the course of 26. DRESDEN-MARATHON – Marathon 42.3 km like?

The course is flat, with 4.7 m of climbing per km on average. It has one climb of at least 25 m, at km 1.0: 32 m over 5.5 km. It runs from the start to a finish in another place. The surface is road.

Are there aid stations at 26. DRESDEN-MARATHON – Marathon 42.3 km?

Yes. The organizer lists 7 aid stations: km 9.0 (water, sports drink, food); km 13.8 (water, sports drink, food); km 18.8 (water, sports drink, food); km 22.0 (water, sports drink, food); km 24.7 (water, sports drink, food); km 27.3 (water, sports drink, gels, food, salt); km 34.9 (water, sports drink, gels, food, salt).

Are there cut-off times at 26. DRESDEN-MARATHON – Marathon 42.3 km?

Yes, according to the organizer: km 17.0: 13:10; km 42.3: 16:30.

How should I pace 26. DRESDEN-MARATHON – Marathon 42.3 km?

Aim for an even effort the whole way, and hold back in the first kilometres when your legs feel fresh and the field pulls away. The course is flat, so an even pace per kilometre works well. In Mila you get a pace plan for this course, based on your own form.
