About the race

42km du Mont-Blanc (Marathon du Mont-Blanc) is a trail race in Chamonix-Mont-Blanc, France, over 42 km. The organizer states 2,540 m of climbing.

The organizer lists 3 aid stations, the first at km 13.5 and the last at km 34.2.

In the Mila app, the coach plans your pacing and fueling for this race.
