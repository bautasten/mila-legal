About the course

Angeles Crest 100 Mile Endurance Run is a trail race in Altadena (Loma Alta Park), United States, over 163.1 km. The course climbs 6,087 m and descends 6,087 m in total, 37 m per km, which makes it very hilly. The highest point is 1,886 m above sea level and the lowest 351 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 131.0: 911 m of ascent over 6.5 km at 14.0 % on average, up to 1,722 m. In total the course has 26 climbs of at least 25 m; the last one starts at km 161.0.

The organizer lists 14 aid stations, the first at km 18.0 and the last at km 155.3.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.
