About the course

Badwater 135 is a road race in Badwater Basin, Death Valley, United States, over 217 km. The course climbs 4,609 m and descends 1,989 m in total, 21 m per km, which makes it hilly. The highest point is 2,543 m above sea level and the lowest -78 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 64.6: 1,510 m of ascent over 30 km at 5.0 % on average, up to 1,510 m. In total the course has 14 climbs of at least 25 m; the last one starts at km 188.9.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.
