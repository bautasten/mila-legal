About the course

Baloise Antwerp 10 Miles is a road race in Antwerpen, Belgium, over 16 km. The course climbs 87 m and descends 91 m in total, 5.4 m per km, which makes it flat. The highest point is 14 m above sea level and the lowest -4 m.

The course has no sustained climbs of 25 m or more.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.

Frequently asked questions

When is Baloise Antwerp 10 Miles 16 km?

How long is Baloise Antwerp 10 Miles 16 km and how much climbing is there?

The race is 16 km with 87 m of climbing and 91 m of descent. The highest point is 14 m above sea level.

What is the course of Baloise Antwerp 10 Miles 16 km like?

The course is flat, with 5.4 m of climbing per km on average. It has no sustained climbs of 25 m or more. It runs from the start to a finish in another place. The surface is road.

How should I pace Baloise Antwerp 10 Miles 16 km?

Aim for an even effort the whole way, and hold back in the first kilometres when your legs feel fresh and the field pulls away. The course is flat, so an even pace per kilometre works well. In Mila you get a pace plan for this course, based on your own form.
