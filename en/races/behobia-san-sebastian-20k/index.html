About the course

Behobia – San Sebastián is a road race in Behobia, Spain, over 20.2 km. The course climbs 293 m and descends 287 m in total, 15 m per km, which makes it gently rolling. The highest point is 100 m above sea level and the lowest 0 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 5.0: 84 m of ascent over 2.4 km at 3.6 % on average, up to 100 m. In total the course has 3 climbs of at least 25 m; the last one starts at km 14.7.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.

Frequently asked questions

When is Behobia – San Sebastián 20.2 km?

Behobia – San Sebastián 20.2 km takes place on Sunday, 8 November 2026. Check the date and start time with the organizer.

How long is Behobia – San Sebastián 20.2 km and how much climbing is there?

The race is 20.2 km with 293 m of climbing and 287 m of descent. The highest point is 100 m above sea level.

What is the course of Behobia – San Sebastián 20.2 km like?

The course is gently rolling, with 15 m of climbing per km on average. It has 3 climbs of at least 25 m; the biggest starts at km 5.0: 84 m over 2.4 km. It runs from the start to a finish in another place. The surface is road.

How should I pace Behobia – San Sebastián 20.2 km?

Aim for an even effort rather than an even pace: let the pace drop on the climbs, win it back where it levels out, and hold back in the first kilometres. The biggest climbs start at km 0.5, km 5.0 and km 14.7. In Mila you get a pace plan for this course, based on your own form.
