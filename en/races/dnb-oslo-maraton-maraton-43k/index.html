About the course

DNB Oslo Maraton – Maraton is a road race in Oslo, Norway, over 42.7 km. The course climbs 267 m and descends 267 m in total, 6.2 m per km, which makes it gently rolling. The highest point is 59 m above sea level and the lowest 2 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 1.9: 47 m of ascent over 3.3 km at 1.4 % on average, up to 59 m. In total the course has 4 climbs of at least 25 m; the last one starts at km 29.7.

Frequently asked questions

When is DNB Oslo Maraton – Maraton 42.7 km?

What time does DNB Oslo Maraton – Maraton 42.7 km start?

How long is DNB Oslo Maraton – Maraton 42.7 km and how much climbing is there?

The race is 42.7 km with 267 m of climbing and 267 m of descent. The highest point is 59 m above sea level.

What is the course of DNB Oslo Maraton – Maraton 42.7 km like?

The course is gently rolling, with 6.2 m of climbing per km on average. It has 4 climbs of at least 25 m; the biggest starts at km 1.9: 47 m over 3.3 km. Start and finish are in the same place. The surface is road.

How should I pace DNB Oslo Maraton – Maraton 42.7 km?

Aim for an even effort rather than an even pace: let the pace drop on the climbs, win it back where it levels out, and hold back in the first kilometres. The biggest climbs start at km 1.9, km 8.3, km 23.2 and km 29.7. In Mila you get a pace plan for this course, based on your own form.
