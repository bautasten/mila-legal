About the course

EDP Maratona de Lisboa is a road race in Carcavelos (Cascais), Portugal, over 42.4 km. The course climbs 389 m and descends 396 m in total, 9.2 m per km, which makes it gently rolling. The highest point is 40 m above sea level and the lowest -1 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 21.5: 26 m of ascent over 2.9 km at 0.9 % on average, up to 26 m. In total the course has 2 climbs of at least 25 m; the last one starts at km 21.5.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.
