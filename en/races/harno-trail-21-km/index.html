The course

About the course

Härnö Trail 21 km is a trail race in Härnösand, Sweden, over 21.1 km. The course climbs 826 m and descends 826 m in total, 39 m per km, which makes it very hilly. The highest point is 176 m above sea level and the lowest 1 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 13.7: 115 m of ascent over 1.4 km at 8.2 % on average, up to 124 m. In total the course has 12 climbs of at least 25 m; the last one starts at km 19.6.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.

Frequently asked questions

When is Härnö Trail 21 km?

Härnö Trail 21 km takes place on Saturday, 2 October 2027 (preliminary date). Check the date and start time with the organizer.

How long is Härnö Trail 21 km and how much climbing is there?

The race is 21.1 km with 826 m of climbing and 826 m of descent. The highest point is 176 m above sea level.

What is the course of Härnö Trail 21 km like?

The course is very hilly, with 39 m of climbing per km on average. It has 12 climbs of at least 25 m; the biggest starts at km 13.7: 115 m over 1.4 km. Start and finish are in the same place. The surface is trail.

How should I pace Härnö Trail 21 km?

Aim for an even effort rather than an even pace: let the pace drop on the climbs, win it back where it levels out, and hold back in the first kilometres. The biggest climbs start at km 1.5, km 6.9, km 13.7 and km 15.9. In Mila you get a pace plan for this course, based on your own form.
