About the course

Härnö Trail 21 km is a trail race in Härnösand, Sweden, over 21.1 km. The course climbs 826 m and descends 826 m in total, 39 m per km, which makes it very hilly. The highest point is 176 m above sea level and the lowest 1 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 13.7: 115 m of ascent over 1.4 km at 8.2 % on average, up to 124 m. In total the course has 12 climbs of at least 25 m; the last one starts at km 19.6.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.
