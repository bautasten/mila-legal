About the course

Haspa Marathon Hamburg (Marathon) is a road race in Hamburg, Germany, over 42.6 km. The course climbs 142 m and descends 141 m in total, 3.3 m per km, which makes it flat. The highest point is 39 m above sea level and the lowest 2 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 2.0: 26 m of ascent over 2.9 km at 0.9 % on average, up to 39 m.
