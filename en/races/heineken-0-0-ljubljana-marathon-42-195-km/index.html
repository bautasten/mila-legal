About the course

Heineken 0.0 Ljubljana Marathon (42,195 km) is a road race in Ljubljana, Slovenia, over 42.3 km. The course climbs 96 m and descends 97 m in total, 2.3 m per km, which makes it flat. The highest point is 315 m above sea level and the lowest 285 m.

The course has no sustained climbs of 25 m or more.

The organizer lists 14 aid stations, the first at km 5.0 and the last at km 40.0.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.
