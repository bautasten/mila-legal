The course

About the course

HOKA Helsingborg Half Marathon is a 21.1 km road race in Helsingborg, and its one notable climb comes straight from the start: 36 m over the first 2.9 km at an average of 1.2 %, topping out at 40 m above sea level. Beyond that, the course gathers its remaining ascent in shorter pieces. It reaches a high point of 52 m and drops as low as 1 m, for 103 m up and 107 m down in total.

The opening is where this race asks for patience. A long, steady rise on fresh legs and race adrenaline makes it easy to go out too hard. Run it by feel and let the effort settle as the gradient eases, so you have something left for the second half. There are 6 aid stations, at km 2.4, 4.7, 8.9, 11.3, 15.8 and 18.8, and all of them have water and sports drink. Four also have food, and km 8.9 adds salt. The longest gaps, 4.2 km and 4.5 km, fall after km 4.7 and km 11.3, so it pays to drink at those stations.

Frequently asked questions

When is Helsingborg Half Marathon 21 km?

Helsingborg Half Marathon 21 km takes place on Saturday, 4 September 2027. Check the date and start time with the organizer.

What time does Helsingborg Half Marathon 21 km start?

According to the organizer: 09:30.

How long is Helsingborg Half Marathon 21 km and how much climbing is there?

The race is 21.1 km with 103 m of climbing and 107 m of descent. The highest point is 52 m above sea level.

What is the course of Helsingborg Half Marathon 21 km like?

The course is flat, with 4.9 m of climbing per km on average. It has one climb of at least 25 m, at km 0.0: 36 m over 2.9 km. It runs from the start to a finish in another place. The surface is road.

Are there aid stations at Helsingborg Half Marathon 21 km?

Yes. The organizer lists 6 aid stations: km 2.4 (water, sports drink, food); km 4.7 (water, sports drink); km 8.9 (water, sports drink, food, salt); km 11.3 (water, sports drink); km 15.8 (water, sports drink, food); km 18.8 (water, sports drink, food).

How should I pace Helsingborg Half Marathon 21 km?

Aim for an even effort the whole way, and hold back in the first kilometres when your legs feel fresh and the field pulls away. The course is flat, so an even pace per kilometre works well. In Mila you get a pace plan for this course, based on your own form.
