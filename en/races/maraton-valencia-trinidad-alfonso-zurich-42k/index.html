About the course

Maratón Valencia Trinidad Alfonso Zurich is a road race in Valencia, Spain, over 42.2 km. The course climbs 249 m and descends 253 m in total, 5.9 m per km, which makes it flat. The highest point is 32 m above sea level and the lowest 1 m.

The course has no sustained climbs of 25 m or more.

There are 2 cut-off times along the course.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.
