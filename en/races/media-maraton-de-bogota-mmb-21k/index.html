The course

About the course

Media Maratón de Bogotá (mmB) – 21K is a road race in Bogotá, Colombia, over 21.3 km. The course climbs 411 m and descends 406 m in total, 19 m per km, which makes it hilly. The highest point is 2,638 m above sea level and the lowest 2,545 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 1.4: 51 m of ascent over 4.7 km at 1.1 % on average, up to 2,610 m. In total the course has 2 climbs of at least 25 m; the last one starts at km 11.0.

The organizer lists 9 aid stations, the first at km 3.0 and the last at km 19.0.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.

How long is Media Maratón de Bogotá (mmB) – 21K and how much climbing is there?

The race is 21.3 km with 411 m of climbing and 406 m of descent. The highest point is 2,638 m above sea level.

What is the course of Media Maratón de Bogotá (mmB) – 21K like?

The course is hilly, with 19 m of climbing per km on average. It has 2 climbs of at least 25 m; the biggest starts at km 1.4: 51 m over 4.7 km. It runs from the start to a finish in another place. The surface is road.

How should I pace Media Maratón de Bogotá (mmB) – 21K?

Aim for an even effort rather than an even pace: let the pace drop on the climbs, win it back where it levels out, and hold back in the first kilometres. The biggest climbs start at km 1.4 and km 11.0. In Mila you get a pace plan for this course, based on your own form.
