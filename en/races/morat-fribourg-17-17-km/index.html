About the course

Morat-Fribourg 17.17 km is a road race in Murten (Morat), Switzerland, over 17.2 km. The course climbs 415 m and descends 253 m in total, 24 m per km, which makes it hilly. The highest point is 620 m above sea level and the lowest 438 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 2.5: 73 m of ascent over 1.6 km at 4.7 % on average, up to 548 m. In total the course has 7 climbs of at least 25 m; the last one starts at km 16.9.

The organizer lists 3 aid stations, the first at km 5.0 and the last at km 13.5.

Frequently asked questions

When is Morat-Fribourg 17.17 km?

Morat-Fribourg 17.17 km takes place on Sunday, 3 October 2027. Check the date and start time with the organizer.

What time does Morat-Fribourg 17.17 km start?

According to the organizer: 10:13.

How long is Morat-Fribourg 17.17 km and how much climbing is there?

The race is 17.2 km with 415 m of climbing and 253 m of descent. The highest point is 620 m above sea level.

What is the course of Morat-Fribourg 17.17 km like?

The course is hilly, with 24 m of climbing per km on average. It has 7 climbs of at least 25 m; the biggest starts at km 2.5: 73 m over 1.6 km. It runs from the start to a finish in another place. The surface is road.

Are there aid stations at Morat-Fribourg 17.17 km?

Yes. The organizer lists 3 aid stations: km 5.0 (water); km 8.0 (water, sports drink, food); km 13.5 (water, sports drink, food).

How should I pace Morat-Fribourg 17.17 km?

Aim for an even effort rather than an even pace: let the pace drop on the climbs, win it back where it levels out, and hold back in the first kilometres. The biggest climbs start at km 2.5, km 4.1, km 6.1 and km 11.8. In Mila you get a pace plan for this course, based on your own form.
