About the course

Nationale-Nederlanden San Silvestre Vallecana (carrera popular 10 km) is a road race in Madrid, Spain, over 10 km. The course climbs 90 m and descends 162 m in total, 9.0 m per km, which makes it gently rolling. The highest point is 719 m above sea level and the lowest 609 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 7.5: 25 m of ascent over 1.1 km at 2.2 % on average, up to 639 m.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.

Frequently asked questions

When is Nationale-Nederlanden San Silvestre Vallecana (carrera popular 10 km)?

Nationale-Nederlanden San Silvestre Vallecana (carrera popular 10 km) takes place on Thursday, 31 December 2026 (preliminary date). Check the date and start time with the organizer.

How long is Nationale-Nederlanden San Silvestre Vallecana (carrera popular 10 km) and how much climbing is there?

The race is 10 km with 90 m of climbing and 162 m of descent. The highest point is 719 m above sea level.

What is the course of Nationale-Nederlanden San Silvestre Vallecana (carrera popular 10 km) like?

The course is gently rolling, with 9.0 m of climbing per km on average. It has one climb of at least 25 m, at km 7.5: 25 m over 1.1 km. It runs from the start to a finish in another place. The surface is road.

How should I pace Nationale-Nederlanden San Silvestre Vallecana (carrera popular 10 km)?

Aim for an even effort rather than an even pace: let the pace drop on the climbs, win it back where it levels out, and hold back in the first kilometres. The biggest climbs start at km 7.5. In Mila you get a pace plan for this course, based on your own form.
