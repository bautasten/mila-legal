About the course

adidas Premiärmilen is a road race in Stockholm (Norra Djurgården), Sweden, over 10 km. The course climbs 64 m and descends 65 m in total, 6.4 m per km, which makes it gently rolling. The highest point is 29 m above sea level and the lowest 1 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 0.7: 25 m of ascent over 880 m at 2.9 % on average, up to 29 m.

The organizer lists 2 aid stations, the first at km 4.6 and the last at km 7.6.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.

Frequently asked questions

When is Premiärmilen 10 km?

Premiärmilen 10 km takes place on Saturday, 3 April 2027. Check the date and start time with the organizer.

What time does Premiärmilen 10 km start?

According to the organizer: 11:00.

How long is Premiärmilen 10 km and how much climbing is there?

The race is 10 km with 64 m of climbing and 65 m of descent. The highest point is 29 m above sea level.

What is the course of Premiärmilen 10 km like?

The course is gently rolling, with 6.4 m of climbing per km on average. It has one climb of at least 25 m, at km 0.7: 25 m over 880 m. It runs from the start to a finish in another place. The surface is road.

Are there aid stations at Premiärmilen 10 km?

Yes. The organizer lists 2 aid stations: km 4.6 (water, sports drink); km 7.6 (water, sports drink).

How should I pace Premiärmilen 10 km?

Aim for an even effort rather than an even pace: let the pace drop on the climbs, win it back where it levels out, and hold back in the first kilometres. The biggest climbs start at km 0.7. In Mila you get a pace plan for this course, based on your own form.
