About the course

Tel Aviv Marathon 2027 – Marathon 42,2 km is a road race in Tel Aviv, Israel, over 42.7 km. The course climbs 451 m and descends 450 m in total, 11 m per km, which makes it gently rolling. The highest point is 38 m above sea level and the lowest 1 m.

The course has no sustained climbs of 25 m or more.
