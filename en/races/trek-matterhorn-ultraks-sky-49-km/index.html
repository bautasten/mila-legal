About the course

TREK Matterhorn Ultraks – SKY 49 km is a trail race in Zermatt, Switzerland, over 49.2 km. The course climbs 3,606 m and descends 3,606 m in total, 73 m per km, which makes it mountainous. The highest point is 3,117 m above sea level and the lowest 1,606 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 9.8: 1,082 m of ascent over 6.2 km at 17.3 % on average, up to 3,117 m. In total the course has 12 climbs of at least 25 m; the last one starts at km 42.9.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.

Frequently asked questions

When is TREK Matterhorn Ultraks – SKY 49 km?

The date of the next edition is not set yet. The race was last held on 22 August 2026.

How long is TREK Matterhorn Ultraks – SKY 49 km and how much climbing is there?

The race is 49.2 km with 3,606 m of climbing and 3,606 m of descent. The highest point is 3,117 m above sea level.

What is the course of TREK Matterhorn Ultraks – SKY 49 km like?

The course is mountainous, with 73 m of climbing per km on average. It has 12 climbs of at least 25 m; the biggest starts at km 9.8: 1,082 m over 6.2 km. Start and finish are in the same place. The surface is trail.

How should I pace TREK Matterhorn Ultraks – SKY 49 km?

Aim for an even effort rather than an even pace: let the pace drop on the climbs, win it back where it levels out, and hold back in the first kilometres. The biggest climbs start at km 0.0, km 9.8, km 27.9 and km 35.5. In Mila you get a pace plan for this course, based on your own form.
