About the course

Umåker Trail 15,6 km is a trail race in Umeå (Röbäck/Umåker), Sweden, over 14.5 km. The course climbs 339 m and descends 312 m in total, 23 m per km, which makes it hilly. The highest point is 122 m above sea level and the lowest 6 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 0.0: 61 m of ascent over 3.3 km at 1.8 % on average, up to 66 m. In total the course has 7 climbs of at least 25 m; the last one starts at km 13.0.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.

Frequently asked questions

When is Umåker Trail 15,6 km?

Umåker Trail 15,6 km takes place on Saturday, 29 May 2027 (preliminary date). Check the date and start time with the organizer.

How long is Umåker Trail 15,6 km and how much climbing is there?

The race is 14.5 km with 339 m of climbing and 312 m of descent. The highest point is 122 m above sea level.

What is the course of Umåker Trail 15,6 km like?

The course is hilly, with 23 m of climbing per km on average. It has 7 climbs of at least 25 m; the biggest starts at km 0.0: 61 m over 3.3 km. It runs from the start to a finish in another place. The surface is trail.

How should I pace Umåker Trail 15,6 km?

Aim for an even effort rather than an even pace: let the pace drop on the climbs, win it back where it levels out, and hold back in the first kilometres. The biggest climbs start at km 0.0, km 6.7, km 7.5 and km 13.0. In Mila you get a pace plan for this course, based on your own form.
