About the course

Venicemarathon 42K is a road race in Stra (Villa Pisani), Italy, over 42.5 km. The course climbs 101 m and descends 107 m in total, 2.4 m per km, which makes it flat. The highest point is 12 m above sea level and the lowest -4 m.

The course has no sustained climbs of 25 m or more.

The organizer lists 8 aid stations, the first at km 5.0 and the last at km 40.0.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.
