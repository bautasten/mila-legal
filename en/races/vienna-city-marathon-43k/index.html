About the course

Vienna City Marathon is a road race in Wien, Austria, over 42.7 km. The course climbs 98 m and descends 87 m in total, 2.3 m per km, which makes it flat. The highest point is 203 m above sea level and the lowest 158 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 1.0: 39 m of ascent over 17 km at 0.2 % on average, up to 203 m.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.

Frequently asked questions

When is Vienna City Marathon 42.7 km?

Vienna City Marathon 42.7 km takes place on Sunday, 18 April 2027. Check the date and start time with the organizer.

What time does Vienna City Marathon 42.7 km start?

According to the organizer: 09:00.

How long is Vienna City Marathon 42.7 km and how much climbing is there?

The race is 42.7 km with 98 m of climbing and 87 m of descent. The highest point is 203 m above sea level.

What is the course of Vienna City Marathon 42.7 km like?

The course is flat, with 2.3 m of climbing per km on average. It has one climb of at least 25 m, at km 1.0: 39 m over 17 km. It runs from the start to a finish in another place. The surface is road.

How should I pace Vienna City Marathon 42.7 km?

Aim for an even effort the whole way, and hold back in the first kilometres when your legs feel fresh and the field pulls away. The course is flat, so an even pace per kilometre works well. In Mila you get a pace plan for this course, based on your own form.
