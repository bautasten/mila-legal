About the course

Winner Jerusalem Marathon – Marathon 42.2 km is a road race in Jerusalem, Israel, over 43 km. The course climbs 1,290 m and descends 1,314 m in total, 30 m per km, which makes it very hilly. The highest point is 827 m above sea level and the lowest 718 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 15.4: 54 m of ascent over 1.1 km at 4.7 % on average, up to 796 m. In total the course has 19 climbs of at least 25 m; the last one starts at km 41.5.

When is Winner Jerusalem Marathon – Marathon 42.2 km 43 km?

What time does Winner Jerusalem Marathon – Marathon 42.2 km 43 km start?

How long is Winner Jerusalem Marathon – Marathon 42.2 km 43 km and how much climbing is there?

The race is 43 km with 1,290 m of climbing and 1,314 m of descent. The highest point is 827 m above sea level.

What is the course of Winner Jerusalem Marathon – Marathon 42.2 km 43 km like?

The course is very hilly, with 30 m of climbing per km on average. It has 19 climbs of at least 25 m; the biggest starts at km 15.4: 54 m over 1.1 km. It runs from the start to a finish in another place. The surface is road.

How should I pace Winner Jerusalem Marathon – Marathon 42.2 km 43 km?

Aim for an even effort rather than an even pace: let the pace drop on the climbs, win it back where it levels out, and hold back in the first kilometres. The biggest climbs start at km 9.2, km 15.4, km 27.1 and km 38.6. In Mila you get a pace plan for this course, based on your own form.
