About the course

Altra Yorkshire Marathon is a road race in York, United Kingdom, over 42.5 km. The course climbs 136 m and descends 146 m in total, 3.2 m per km, which makes it flat. The highest point is 42 m above sea level and the lowest 9 m.

The biggest climb starts at km 22.3: 26 m of ascent over 4.3 km at 0.6 % on average, up to 38 m. In total the course has 2 climbs of at least 25 m; the last one starts at km 22.3.

In the Mila app, the coach describes the course in detail in your language and plans your pacing and fueling for it.
