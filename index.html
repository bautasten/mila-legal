Apple Watch

Mila on your wrist

Mila's Apple Watch app shows your day, records your race and asks how your run went. Other watches work through Apple Health.

Today on your watchThe coach's call and today's session on your wrist, with complications and the Smart Stack. Start today's session and tell your coach right from the watch, and after a run it asks "How did the run go?".
Race modeThe watch records the race without your phone, with pace and fueling cues, climb alerts and the course on the watch. The race is saved to Apple Health like any other run.
Runs in the Workout appThe coach's runs sit in the Workout app in their own row called Mila, with heart rate or pace alerts between warm-up and cool-down. The run comes back on its own through Apple Health.

Everything a runner needs

From the first easy run to race day.

Weekly plan and analysis

A weekly plan with strength programs and sessions you can move. After the run, planned vs. completed, the route colored by pace and similar runs. Your patterns, Compared with, records over time and My running year.

Mila's race directory

Official races with map, elevation profile, aid stations and the organizer's link – or upload the course yourself (GPX, KML, TCX or zip). Every race in the directory also has its own page: milacoach.app/en/races.

Before and after the race

A forecast, a pace plan for the hills, a fueling plan, Am I ready?, a race guide and race morning. Afterwards, splits against the plan, the coach's race analysis and your official result.

Running form

Film in the app or pick a video. Only still frames are sent, with faces blurred, and the video never leaves your phone. The coach gives observations and drills, woven into your plan and followed up over time.

Shoes

The coach judges how long each shoe lasts for you – roughly how many kilometers and weeks at your pace. Import your shoe log from a picture or a CSV file.

Food

Photograph or describe a meal, and the coach estimates it and links it to your training. Correct it in your own words, show it without numbers, or log with Siri.

Share

Images of runs and races with a backdrop in Mila's style from the place, a suggested text and a line from the coach.

In your calendar

The coach's runs in the calendar you choose, at the best time for the weather.

Your watch

Works with your watch

Mila reads your runs from Apple Health, so you don't need a particular watch.

Apple WatchMila's own app on the watch with Today and race mode, and the coach's runs in the Workout app with warm-up, intervals and cool-down.
Other watchesGarmin, COROS, Suunto, Polar and other watches and rings work when the watch's app syncs to Apple Health. Mila reads your runs and, depending on the watch, heart rate, sleep and resting heart rate. The coach's sessions don't appear on these watches – you follow the plan in the app. Polar can also be connected directly.
No watchRecord your runs with your iPhone, for example in the Workout app, so they land in Apple Health.

Your data stays yours

Apple Health data is read on your phone; a summary goes to the coach, after your consent, together with what you connect or send (Polar, meal photos, pictures of your shoe log, still frames from running-form videos). Your account is stored in the EU (Ireland). The coach's texts are written by an external AI service in the USA under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Mila never sells your data or uses it for advertising, and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time. Mila gives general training advice, not medical advice.
