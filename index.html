Coach at hand

A coach one tap away

Every morning the coach writes what today is for and why, with the numbers that matter. When the week ends – Sunday evening, or by Monday morning – the weekly review and next week's plan arrive. And when you wonder about something, ask the coach directly – the answers know your data.

Apple Watch

Today's run on your watch

The coach's runs land in the Workout app on your Apple Watch, and everything you run comes back on its own through Apple Health.

In the Workout appThe coach's runs sit on your watch as their own row called Mila, with heart rate or pace alerts between warm-up and cool-down.
Back on its ownThe run comes back through Apple Health with heart rate and running dynamics, and the coach also sees sleep, HRV and VO₂max. No export, no cable, no third-party login.
Works with any watch via Apple HealthApple Watch is optional. Other watches and rings that sync to Apple Health work too; then you follow the plan in the app. And just your phone works as well.

Your watch

Works with your watch

Mila reads your runs from Apple Health, so you don't need a particular watch.

Apple WatchWorks directly. The coach's sessions can also be put on the watch, with warm-up, intervals and cool-down.
Other watchesGarmin, COROS, Suunto, Polar, Amazfit, WHOOP, Oura and any other watch or ring work when the watch's app syncs to Apple Health. Mila reads your runs and, depending on the watch, heart rate, sleep and resting heart rate. The coach's sessions don't appear on these watches – you follow the plan in the app. Polar can also be connected directly. Works with COROS: your COROS account can be connected directly in the app too, with “Powered by COROS” and your watch model next to its data. Strava runs come in through Apple Health.
No watchRecord your runs with your iPhone, for example in the Workout app, so they land in Apple Health.

Your data stays yours

Apple Health data is read on your phone; a summary goes to the coach, plus what you connect or send (Polar, COROS, meal photos, still frames from running-form films). Mila never sells your data or uses it for advertising, and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time. Your account is stored in the EU; the coach's texts are written by an external AI service in the USA, only after your consent. Mila gives training advice, not medical advice.
