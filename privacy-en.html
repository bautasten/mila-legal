Privacy policy

Mila · Updated 2026-10-14 · Terms of use · På svenska · Auf Deutsch

Who is responsible

Mila is published by Patrik Berggren, who is the controller for the personal data described here. Contact: the contact form at https://milacoach.app/support.html.

What Mila reads on your phone

With your permission Mila reads health data from Apple Health: workouts (with their route), heart rate, HRV, resting heart rate, VO₂max, sleep, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, sleeping wrist temperature, weight, height, body composition, activity, walking data, nutrition you log in other apps (energy, protein, carbohydrates and fat), and your date of birth and biological sex (Mila uses your age, not the date). This data is read and analysed on your phone. Mila on your phone never writes to Apple Health. Only when you use race mode in Mila on Apple Watch does the watch save the race as a workout in Apple Health, with its route, distance, active energy and heart rate; the watch sends it nowhere else, and it reaches the coach like any other run, through the summary. If you turn on sessions on Apple Watch, Mila puts the coach's runs in the Workout app on your watch; that happens on the phone and nothing about it goes through Mila's server. The same goes for the coach's sessions in your calendar if you turn that on: Mila writes only its own events to the calendar you choose and reads no others.

What leaves your phone

To write your coach texts, Mila sends a short summary of your training and health (for example weekly distance, recent workouts, form, recovery, sleep, blood oxygen, body measurements, age and sex, food you have logged and the day's weather forecast) together with your own description of yourself and your goal to Mila's server. The summary is used only to write the text: weekly texts keep it in a queue until they are written, at most about a day, and then it is cleared. The coach texts, your description, your goal and a coach profile derived from it are stored on your account. Sessions you tick off in the plan are stored on your account with their date, minutes and perceived effort (1–5), so the coach and all your phones see them; they are deleted with the account. What you write to the coach in your own words under Change the week, and the coach's short interpretation of it (what has happened, a short summary and for how many days it affects your training), are stored on your account until you delete the account, and are sent to the external AI service as part of the summary the coach writes from. Shoes you add (name, date, distance, replacement limit and the coach's details on the model), which run was done in which shoe and which shoe was your default, notes you write on your workouts, and races you add to your plan with the courses you upload for them are stored on your account, so they follow you to a new phone; they are deleted with the account. If you connect Polar, Mila stores the access tokens and, for the data types you approve at Polar: training sessions (with heart rate, speed and route), sleep (time asleep, sleep stages and sleep score), Nightly Recharge (night-time HRV, heart rate, breathing rate and recovery status), continuous heart rate (used for your daily resting heart rate), daily activity (steps and active time), night-time skin temperature, test results (orthostatic test, fitness test, SpO2) and diary entries in your Polar calendar (weight, feeling, perceived recovery and your physical settings such as maximum heart rate, resting heart rate, thresholds and VO2max). From your Polar physical settings Mila stores only max HR, resting HR, thresholds, VO₂max and FTP; birthday, sex and height are not read or stored. Mila does not read your e-mail, devices or diary notes at Polar, and cannot send anything to your Polar watch. If you allow notifications, Mila stores your phone's push address, with the phone's language, time zone and region, to tell you when weekly texts are ready (the language and time zone set the notification's text and time of day; the region is used to count users per country). Race courses you upload from a file or link are added to Mila's shared race registry, without your name. If you import workouts from files you exported yourself (FIT, GPX or TCX, for example Strava's archive), Mila reads the files on your phone and stores the workouts on your account: time, activity, distance, elevation gain, energy, heart rate (average, maximum and heart rate curve), speed and GPS route. The file itself is not sent to Mila's server and is not kept. Workouts you add manually are stored on your account with activity, time, distance, perceived effort (1–5) and your note. You can delete such a workout in the app; all of them are deleted with the account. Mila does not connect to Strava; workouts you record in Strava reach Mila through Apple Health or from files you export yourself.

Subscription

If you subscribe to Mila Coach, Mila stores the App Store transaction id, the product, the status and the expiry date on your account to know whether the subscription is active. Apple sends Mila updates when the subscription renews, ends or is refunded. Payment details are handled by Apple and never reach Mila.

Meal photos

A meal photo is sent to the AI model for assessment and then discarded. Mila stores only the estimated numbers and text, never the photo.

AI support

Messages you write to the support in the app are answered by an AI (an external AI service), and the answer says so. With your message the app sends a few technical details about the app and its connections (such as app version, iOS version and which services are connected), not your health data. The Mila team may read your support conversation, for up to 180 days, to help you and to improve the support. If the AI cannot help, or you ask for a person, a copy of the conversation is also e-mailed to Mila's support inbox. The conversation is stored on your account for 180 days and then deleted. If a person at Mila answers, the answer appears in the same conversation. When you delete your account, the conversation and the copies of it stored by Mila are deleted; a copy already e-mailed to the support inbox may remain there for up to 90 days.

Ask the coach

Questions you ask under "Ask the coach" are sent, with a summary of your training data and your most recent questions and answers, to an external AI service to write the answer. The questions and answers are kept only on your phone (the latest 20) and are deleted when you sign out. Mila's server keeps only the latest answer for about ten minutes, so it can be fetched again if the connection drops, and a count of how many questions you have asked. If you leave the app before the answer is ready, it is kept until the app has fetched it, at most two days, and you get a notification without the answer in it.

Running form analysis

If you ask the coach to analyse your running form, your films are read on your phone and never leave it. The phone takes out still frames at key moments of your stride (at most 8 per angle), together with estimated measurements, and sends them to Mila's server. The frames wait in a queue until the analysis has been written, usually within a day and at most a few days if it has to be tried again, and are then deleted at Mila; the batch with them is deleted at the AI service once Mila has collected the analysis. The written analysis is stored on your account so the coach can compare it with the next one. The analysis is a coach's observations, not a medical assessment, and does not replace a running analysis in a lab.

Location and weather

Your current location is used on the phone to fetch the weather forecast from Apple Weather and is not stored; your location and the name of the place are never sent to Mila's server or the AI service. In race mode, Mila on Apple Watch uses the watch's GPS for the race's route, which is saved with the workout in Apple Health. The forecast itself (temperature, rain and wind) is part of the summary the coach writes from. Workouts fetched from Polar include their GPS route, which is stored with the workout on your account until you disconnect or delete the account. The GPS route of a workout you import from a file is stored with the workout on your account until you delete the workout or the account.

Sign in

You sign in with Apple. Mila receives an anonymous identifier from Apple and, if you choose to share it, your email address. Mila also keeps a token from Apple on the server, used only to withdraw the sign-in when you delete your account; it is deleted with the account. If Apple gives Mila your first name, it is kept only on your phone, as is any photo you choose under More → Account; neither is sent to Mila's server.

Who processes the data

Supabase (database and server functions, hosted in the EU, Ireland) stores your account data. Only after you have agreed to it in the app, an external AI service (sub-processor) in the USA processes the training summary (including data from a connected Polar account: training sessions, sleep, Nightly Recharge, heart rate, activity and test results, and workouts you import or add manually, with your note), your description, meal photos, meals you describe, race course files you add (start, finish and place), what you tell the coach about yourself and your workouts, and the still frames for a running form analysis to write coach texts, your questions to Ask the coach to answer them, and your messages to the AI support to answer them, as well as the name, place, date and distance of the races in your plan (nothing about you) to find the race organiser's official website and the race's official aid stations, and to summarise the results of recent years (up to five editions) from the race's public results lists into the number of finishers and finishing times (no names are stored), which the coach compares with your forecast and, for your class, with your age and sex to estimate your placing; the same details of a race that is not yet in Mila's race registry are also used to find the course the organiser has published, which is then added to the registry with no link to you; the AI service does not use the data to train its models. The transfer to the USA is protected by the EU's standard contractual clauses in the AI service's data processing terms. Weekly texts are written through the AI service's batch service, and each batch is deleted there once Mila has collected the texts. Apple provides sign-in, Apple Health, weather, push notifications (Apple Push Notification service, APNs: your phone's push address and the notification text) and payment for the subscription. Resend (USA) delivers e-mail: support conversations the AI support forwards to a person, and messages sent through the support form on Mila's website, are e-mailed to Mila's support inbox through Resend. Open-Meteo (Switzerland) receives only the coordinates of a race course that lacks elevation, to look up its elevation. Polar only if you connect it. The full list of sub-processors is available on request through the contact form on Mila's website.

Your consent to the AI coach

Before anything is sent to the AI service, Mila asks for your explicit consent in the app: the coach's texts, plans and answers are written by an external AI service (sub-processor) in the USA, and what it receives is listed there (a summary of your training and health data, your description and goal, meal photos you take and meals you describe, still frames from running-form films, race course files you add (start, finish and place), workouts you import or add manually with your note and perceived effort, what you tell the coach about yourself and your workouts, your questions to Ask the coach and your messages to the support in the app). If you choose Not now, the app works without the coach and nothing is sent to the AI; the support can then be reached through the contact form on Mila's website. You can withdraw your consent at any time under More → Account → AI coach: from then on nothing more is sent to the AI service and no new coach texts are written, while texts already written stay on your account until you delete it. You can give consent again in the same place.

What Mila never does

Mila never sells your data and shares it only with the services that run Mila (among them an external AI service in the USA that writes the coach's texts), as listed above. Health data is never stored in iCloud, never used for advertising or marketing and never shared with data brokers. Health data from Apple Health is only used to provide the coaching in the app.

How long data is kept

Data is kept while you have an account. Coach texts older than two years may be deleted. Conversations with the AI support are deleted after 180 days; the copy of one forwarded to a person is kept in the support inbox for at most 90 days. Messages sent through the support form on Mila's website are kept in Mila's database for 90 days, with the e-mail address you give and a one-way code (hash) of the sender's IP address to stop spam, and are then deleted. When you delete your account under More → Account, all data on your account is deleted from Mila's server immediately: description, coach texts, goal, what you told the coach under Change the week, connections, fetched workouts, imported and manually added workouts, ticked sessions, shoes, workout notes, races and courses you added, meals, support conversation and its forwarded copies, push addresses with their language, time zone and region, and subscription status. A copy already e-mailed to the support inbox may remain there for up to 90 days. Two small records are kept apart from the account, under a one-way code of your Apple sign-in and nothing else: the App Store transaction id of a purchase together with that code, so the purchase is returned to the right person and cannot be taken over by someone else, and a count of the coach's free calls with the time you first signed up, so the coach's free start (its first days and free calls) cannot be repeated with a new account. They are kept as long as Mila is offered, at most five years from when they were recorded, and then deleted. Deleting the account does not cancel an App Store subscription: cancel it in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Mila cannot remove its own access at Polar: after you disconnect Polar or delete your account, remove Mila at account.polar.com. Courses you added to the shared race registry remain without any link to you. To limit how much the coach can be used per person, Mila keeps a count of coach requests for nine days (29 days for running form analyses), linked to your account and to a one-way code derived from your Apple sign-in. If you delete your account, the link to the account is removed, but the count stays for the rest of those days so the limit cannot be avoided with a new account; it is then deleted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your data, object to processing, and receive your data in a portable format. Contact us at the address above. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), or to the data protection supervisory authority in the country where you live.

Legal basis

Mila processes health data on the basis of your explicit consent: to Apple Health access, which you give in iOS, and to the AI coach, which you give in the app before anything is sent to the AI service. You can withdraw consent at any time: the AI coach under More → Account, Apple Health access in the Health app, or everything by deleting your account.
