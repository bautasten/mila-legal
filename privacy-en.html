Who is responsible

Mila is published by Patrik Berggren, who is the controller for the personal data described here. Contact: the contact form at https://milacoach.app/support.html.

What Mila reads on your phone

With your permission Mila reads health data from Apple Health: workouts (with their route), heart rate, HRV, resting heart rate, VO₂max, sleep, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, sleeping wrist temperature, weight, height, body composition, activity, walking data, nutrition you log in other apps (energy, protein, carbohydrates and fat), and your date of birth and biological sex (Mila uses your age, not the date). This data is read and analysed on your phone. Mila on your phone never writes to Apple Health. Only when you use race mode in Mila on Apple Watch does the watch save the race as a workout in Apple Health, with its route, distance, active energy and heart rate; the watch sends it nowhere else, and it reaches the coach like any other run, through the summary. If you turn on sessions on Apple Watch, Mila puts the coach's runs in the Workout app on your watch; that happens on the phone and nothing about it goes through Mila's server. The same goes for the coach's sessions in your calendar if you turn that on: Mila writes only its own events to the calendar you choose and reads no others.

What leaves your phone

To write your coach texts, Mila sends a short summary of your training and health (for example weekly distance, recent workouts, form, recovery, sleep, blood oxygen, body measurements, age and sex, food you have logged and the day's weather forecast) together with your own description of yourself and your goal to Mila's server. The summary is used only to write the text: weekly texts keep it in a queue until they are written, at most about a day, and then it is cleared. The coach texts, your description, your goal and a coach profile derived from it are stored on your account. Sessions you tick off in the plan are stored on your account with their date, minutes and perceived effort (1–5), so the coach and all your phones see them; they are deleted with the account. If you move a session, the moved plan is stored on your account, and the plan and the move are sent to the external AI service so the coach can check the week. Exercises you tick off in a strength session and your watch choices (No watch, and whether the warm-up and cool-down go to Apple Watch as open or detailed steps) are stored on your account and deleted with the account. What you write to the coach in your own words under Tell your coach that the coach finds concerns the week, and the coach's short interpretation of it (what has happened, a short summary and for how many days it affects your training), are stored on your account until you delete the account, and are sent to the external AI service as part of the summary the coach writes from. To decide whether what you write under Tell your coach on Today concerns today's session or the week, your words are first sent to the external AI service with a short description of today's session; this is not stored. What you write there that concerns today's session is sent to the external AI service in the same way, with the day's summary, and your words, the coach's answer and your choice (use the suggestion or keep the plan) are stored on your account as a coach text for that day; if you use the suggestion, today's session in your plan is replaced. The days you tick off under This week's focus are stored on your account per week, with the coach's focus and how many days it set, so all your phones see them and the coach sees how it went in the weekly review; they are deleted with the account. Shoes you add (name, date, distance, replacement limit and the coach's details on the model), which run was done in which shoe and which shoe was your default, notes you write on your workouts, and races you add to your plan with the courses you upload for them are stored on your account, so they follow you to a new phone; they are deleted with the account. If you connect Polar, Mila stores the access tokens and, for the data types you approve at Polar: training sessions (with heart rate, speed and route), sleep (time asleep, sleep stages and sleep score), Nightly Recharge (night-time HRV, heart rate, breathing rate and recovery status), continuous heart rate (used for your daily resting heart rate), daily activity (steps and active time), night-time skin temperature, test results (orthostatic test, fitness test, SpO2) and diary entries in your Polar calendar (weight, feeling, perceived recovery and your physical settings such as maximum heart rate, resting heart rate, thresholds and VO2max). From your Polar physical settings Mila stores only max HR, resting HR, thresholds, VO₂max and FTP; birthday, sex and height are not read or stored. Mila does not read your e-mail, devices or diary notes at Polar, and cannot send anything to your Polar watch. If you connect COROS, you first agree in the app to what Mila fetches; Mila then stores the access tokens and, from your COROS account: workouts (time, activity, distance, energy, heart rate and pace, and the watch model), sleep (time asleep, sleep stages and sleep score), night-time HRV, resting heart rate, daily steps and your VO₂max. Mila does not read your COROS profile (height, weight, birthday, sex), your location or GPS tracks, stress or training plans, and does not send anything to your COROS watch. Data from COROS is shown with "Powered by COROS" and the watch model. When you disconnect COROS or delete your account, or when COROS no longer accepts Mila's access, Mila withdraws its access at COROS where it can and deletes all data fetched from COROS at once, in any case within 24 hours. If you allow notifications, Mila stores your phone's push address, with the phone's language, time zone and region, to tell you when weekly texts are ready (the language and time zone set the notification's text and time of day; the region is used to count users per country). The coach's notifications come between 08:00 and 21:00 on your phone; a reply from support (the AI support or a person at Mila) is sent as soon as it is ready, at any hour. For a race in your plan, Mila stores the race's name, date, start time and the coach's breakfast time on your account to send the race-week notifications (the evening before and on race morning); they are removed when the race has passed or left your plan, or when you turn race notifications off under More → Notifications, and are deleted with the account. Race courses you upload from a file or link are added to Mila's shared race registry, without your name. If you import workouts from files you exported yourself (FIT, GPX or TCX, for example Strava's archive), Mila reads the files on your phone and stores the workouts on your account: time, activity, distance, elevation gain, energy, heart rate (average, maximum and heart rate curve), speed and GPS route. The file itself is not sent to Mila's server and is not kept. Workouts you add manually are stored on your account with activity, time, distance, perceived effort (1–5) and your note. You can delete such a workout in the app; all of them are deleted with the account. Mila does not connect to Strava; workouts you record in Strava reach Mila through Apple Health or from files you export yourself.

Subscription

If you subscribe to Mila Coach, Mila stores the App Store transaction id, the product, the status and the expiry date on your account to know whether the subscription is active. Apple sends Mila updates when the subscription renews, ends or is refunded. Payment details are handled by Apple and never reach Mila.

Meal photos

A meal photo is sent to the AI model for assessment and then discarded. Mila stores only the estimated numbers and text, never the photo.

AI support

Messages you write to the support in the app are answered by an AI (an external AI service), and the answer says so. With your message the app sends a few technical details about the app and its connections (such as app version, iOS version and which services are connected), not your health data. The Mila team may read your support conversation, for up to 180 days, to help you and to improve the support. If the AI cannot help, or you ask for a person, a copy of the conversation is also e-mailed to Mila's support inbox. The conversation is stored on your account for 180 days and then deleted. If a person at Mila answers, the answer appears in the same conversation. When you delete your account, the conversation and the copies of it stored by Mila are deleted; a copy already e-mailed to the support inbox may remain there for up to 90 days.

Ask the coach

Questions you ask under "Ask the coach" are sent, with a summary of your training data and your most recent questions and answers, to an external AI service to write the answer. The questions and answers are kept only on your phone (the latest 20) and are deleted when you sign out. Mila's server keeps only the latest answer for about ten minutes, so it can be fetched again if the connection drops, and a count of how many questions you have asked. If you leave the app before the answer is ready, it is kept until the app has fetched it, at most two days, and you get a notification without the answer in it.

Your patterns and the year in review

Once a month the coach looks for patterns in your own data under Analysis → Recovery. A compact summary of the last weeks is then sent to the external AI service, the same kind of data the coach already receives: per day and week your runs (time of day, distance, time, pace, average heart rate, elevation gain, perceived effort and your notes), sleep, HRV and resting heart rate, together with your answers on whether last month's patterns were right. Those answers are kept only on your phone. For the coach's reflection in My running year (in December, or for the previous year when you open it) a summary of the year is sent the same way: distance, time, elevation gain and number of runs per month, races with times, records, the most-used shoe and weeks in a row with running. The patterns and the reflection are stored on your account like other coach texts. Since you can share the reflection, the coach writes it about your training only: it never mentions injuries, illness, weight, heart rate, sleep or other health details.

Shared year pages (My running year)

If you choose Share my year, Mila stores what you chose to show (for example total distance, time and elevation gain, races with times, records, your favourite shoe and, if you turn it on, the coach's reflection, which is then taken on the server from what the coach wrote for the year and never mentions health details) and a picture of your year made in the app, at an address that cannot be guessed. Heart rate, weight and other health values are never included. Anyone with the link can see the page; it is not indexed by search engines. The page is served through Cloudflare at ar.milacoach.app, which sees visitors' requests but nothing about your account; the page loads no fonts, scripts or anything else from third parties, and is never kept in a cache. You can have at most three active links per year and can stop sharing at any time in the app; the page then stops showing at once, and the page and the picture are removed entirely within a few minutes. Everything is deleted when you delete your account. Once a year, from 1 December, you get a notification when your running year is put together.

Running form analysis

If you ask the coach to analyse your running form, your films are read on your phone and never leave it. The phone takes out still frames at key moments of your stride (at most 8 per angle), together with estimated measurements, and sends them to Mila's server. The frames wait in a queue until the analysis has been written, usually within a day and at most a few days if it has to be tried again, and are then deleted at Mila; the batch with them is deleted at the AI service once Mila has collected the analysis. The written analysis is stored on your account so the coach can compare it with the next one. The analysis is a coach's observations, not a medical assessment, and does not replace a running analysis in a lab.

After the race

When a race in your plan has been run, the coach writes an analysis of it: the summary sent to the external AI service then also holds the race's course, your time, your kilometre splits and heart rate during the race, the coach's plan for the race, your own notes on the race (also your answer on the watch after the run), the day's weather at the start and your earlier times in the same race; the analysis is stored on your account with the other coach texts. Only if you ask for it under Get my official result, the name or bib number you type is sent, with the race's name, place, date and distance, to the external AI service to find your own result in the organiser's or timing provider's public results list (the race comes from your own race on your account, not from the app). The service first looks for the race's results list and then for your name or number in the pages it reads; the name may also be used in its web searches for that lookup. What you type is used only for that lookup and is not stored by Mila; Mila stores only your result (official time, place overall and in your class, the class and the results page) on your account, never anyone else's, and it is deleted with the race or the account. A share image you make of a race or a workout is drawn on your phone and goes only where you send it. Heart rate, the coach's comment and the route are included from the start and can be turned off before you share. The backdrop is drawn on your phone too: to choose the landscape (for example city, lake or coast), the phone looks up the place a little way into the route through Apple's location service, and only the answer is kept, on the phone. When you open sharing, Mila asks the coach for a suggested text to go with the image (only if you have agreed to the AI coach): only what the image shows is sent to the external AI service (activity, date, distance, time, pace, elevation, the race's name and place, the official time and placing, the days left and forecast before a race, a new record, and the coach's comment if it is on the image), never heart rate or other health data. The suggestion is stored on your account per image and language so it is not written again, and is deleted with the account. The text you share, which Mila also copies to the clipboard when you share, goes only where you send it.

Elevation for the race directory

Only if you turn on Contribute elevation to the race directory does the app send anything about elevation. The app asks once, when you have run a race from Mila's race directory; the setting is off from the start and is found under More → Watches and apps. With it on, after such a race the app sends only the elevation profile along the race's course: the elevation your watch or phone measured, one height every 25 metres along the course (longer steps on ultra races), and whether an Apple Watch, a phone or something else measured it. No coordinates, times, heart rate or other details are sent. The heights are stored without any link to you or your account and are used only to give the race's course measured elevation instead of elevation from an elevation model, in the app and on Mila's race pages. To limit misuse, Mila counts how many contributions your account has sent in the last 24 hours; that count is kept apart from the heights and deleted after nine days. Since a contribution cannot be linked to you, it cannot be removed afterwards and stays if you delete your account; it goes with the race. Turn the setting off under More → Watches and apps whenever you like, and nothing more is sent.

Location and weather

Your current location is used on the phone to fetch the weather forecast from Apple Weather and is not stored; your location and the name of the place are never sent to Mila's server or the AI service. In race mode, Mila on Apple Watch uses the watch's GPS for the race's route, which is saved with the workout in Apple Health, and to show where you are on the course and how far it is to the next climb, aid station and the finish; that position stays on the watch. The forecast itself (temperature, rain and wind) is part of the summary the coach writes from. Workouts fetched from Polar include their GPS route, which is stored with the workout on your account until you disconnect or delete the account. The GPS route of a workout you import from a file is stored with the workout on your account until you delete the workout or the account. For a workout with GPS, the phone fetches the weather during the workout from Apple Weather, using the workout's start point rounded to about a kilometre and its time; that weather is kept only on the phone. The race-day weather for a race in your plan is fetched the same way, for the course's start rounded to about a kilometre: the weather for a race or a run never uses an exact position. Routes you run again are found on the phone from your workouts' routes, and the routes do not leave it; if you give such a route a name, the name is stored on your account with an id that is the id of the route's first workout, never any coordinates, and is deleted with the account.

Sign in

You sign in with Apple. Mila receives an anonymous identifier from Apple and, if you choose to share it, your email address. Mila also keeps a token from Apple on the server, used only to withdraw the sign-in when you delete your account; it is deleted with the account. If Apple gives Mila your first name, it is kept only on your phone, as is any photo you choose under More → Account; neither is sent to Mila's server.

Who processes the data

Supabase (database and server functions, hosted in the EU, Ireland) stores your account data. Only after you have agreed to it in the app, an external AI service (sub-processor) in the USA processes the training summary (including data from a connected Polar account: training sessions, sleep, Nightly Recharge, heart rate, activity and test results; data from a connected COROS account: workouts, sleep, night-time HRV, resting heart rate, steps and VO₂max; and workouts you import or add manually, with your note), your description, meal photos, meals you describe, race course files you add (start, finish and place), what you tell the coach about yourself and your workouts, and the still frames for a running form analysis to write coach texts, your questions to Ask the coach to answer them, and your messages to the AI support to answer them, as well as the name, place, date and distance of the races in your plan (nothing about you) to find the race organiser's official website and the race's official aid stations, and to summarise the results of recent years (up to five editions) from the race's public results lists into the number of finishers and finishing times (no names are stored), which the coach compares with your forecast and, for your class, with your age and sex to estimate your placing; the same details of a race that is not yet in Mila's race registry are also used to find the course the organiser has published, which is then added to the registry with no link to you; and the name, place, date and distance of a race in your plan (nothing about you) to find the start times, start place, bib pickup, cut-off times and mandatory kit the organiser has published for it; and, only when you ask for your official result, the name or bib number you type with the race's name, place, date and distance, which the AI service may use in its web searches to find your result (Mila does not store what you type); the AI service does not use the data to train its models. The transfer to the USA is protected by the EU's standard contractual clauses in the AI service's data processing terms. Weekly texts are written through the AI service's batch service, and each batch is deleted there once Mila has collected the texts. Apple provides sign-in, Apple Health, weather, push notifications (Apple Push Notification service, APNs: your phone's push address and the notification text) and payment for the subscription. Resend (USA) delivers e-mail: support conversations the AI support forwards to a person, and messages sent through the support form on Mila's website, are e-mailed to Mila's support inbox through Resend. For a race course that lacks elevation, Mila's server looks up the heights of the course's coordinates itself in public elevation map tiles (Terrain Tiles, Amazon Web Services' open data); it only downloads the map tiles that cover the course, and no data about you is sent. Polar and COROS only if you connect them. The full list of sub-processors is available on request through the contact form on Mila's website.

Your consent to the AI coach

Before anything is sent to the AI service, Mila asks for your explicit consent in the app: the coach's texts, plans and answers are written by an external AI service (sub-processor) in the USA, and what it receives is listed there (a summary of your training and health data, your description and goal, meal photos you take and meals you describe, still frames from running-form films, race course files you add (start, finish and place), workouts you import or add manually with your note and perceived effort, what you tell the coach about yourself and your workouts, your questions to Ask the coach and your messages to the support in the app). If you choose Not now, the app works without the coach and nothing is sent to the AI; the support can then be reached through the contact form on Mila's website. You can withdraw your consent at any time under More → Account → AI coach: from then on nothing more is sent to the AI service and no new coach texts are written, while texts already written stay on your account until you delete it. You can give consent again in the same place.

What Mila never does

Mila never sells your data and shares it only with the services that run Mila (among them an external AI service in the USA that writes the coach's texts), as listed above. Health data is never stored in iCloud, never used for advertising or marketing and never shared with data brokers. Health data from Apple Health is only used to provide the coaching in the app.

How long data is kept

Data is kept while you have an account. Coach texts older than two years may be deleted. Conversations with the AI support are deleted after 180 days; the copy of one forwarded to a person is kept in the support inbox for at most 90 days. Messages sent through the support form on Mila's website are kept in Mila's database for 90 days, with the e-mail address you give and a one-way code (hash) of the sender's IP address to stop spam, and are then deleted. When you delete your account under More → Account, all data on your account is deleted from Mila's server immediately: description, coach texts, goal, what you told the coach under Tell your coach, connections, fetched workouts, imported and manually added workouts, ticked sessions, shoes, workout notes, races and courses you added, meals, support conversation and its forwarded copies, push addresses with their language, time zone and region, and subscription status. A copy already e-mailed to the support inbox may remain there for up to 90 days. Two small records are kept apart from the account, under a one-way code of your Apple sign-in and nothing else: the App Store transaction id of a purchase together with that code, so the purchase is returned to the right person and cannot be taken over by someone else, and a count of the coach's free calls with the time you first signed up, so the coach's free start (its first days and free calls) cannot be repeated with a new account. They are kept as long as Mila is offered, at most five years from when they were recorded, and then deleted. Deleting the account does not cancel an App Store subscription: cancel it in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Mila cannot remove its own access at Polar: after you disconnect Polar or delete your account, remove Mila at account.polar.com. Data fetched from COROS is deleted at once when you disconnect COROS or delete your account, and Mila withdraws its access at COROS. Courses you added to the shared race registry remain without any link to you. To limit how much the coach can be used per person, Mila keeps a count of coach requests for nine days (29 days for running form analyses), linked to your account and to a one-way code derived from your Apple sign-in. If you delete your account, the link to the account is removed, but the count stays for the rest of those days so the limit cannot be avoided with a new account; it is then deleted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your data, object to processing, and receive your data in a portable format. Contact us at the address above. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), or to the data protection supervisory authority in the country where you live.

Legal basis

Mila processes health data on the basis of your explicit consent: to Apple Health access, which you give in iOS, and to the AI coach, which you give in the app before anything is sent to the AI service. You can withdraw consent at any time: the AI coach under More → Account, Apple Health access in the Health app, or everything by deleting your account.
