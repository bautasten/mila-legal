Mila – App Review guide

This page belongs to the Notes for Review of the current build. It lists every feature added since build 16, says where each one is in the app and how you can see it with a new test account, and says what needs real data (runs in Apple Health or an Apple Watch). Nothing in Mila is hidden or dormant: every feature below is in this build and on for every subscriber, and More → Help describes them in the app.

The app is in English, Swedish and German; the labels below are the English ones. The five tabs are Today, Training, Analysis, Races and More.

Before you start

  • Sign in with Apple, accept screen A (health data, 18+), choose "All recorded data" when Apple Health asks, agree to the coach's consent, describe yourself and a goal in a few words, and start the free week with your sandbox account. The first week's plan arrives within minutes.
  • A new account has no runs. Most features below show at once or after you type something; the ones that need runs in Apple Health or an Apple Watch say so. If your test device has running workouts in Apple Health, more of them appear.
  • Everything the coach writes is written per runner by the coach (an external AI service in the USA, after consent). The app has no fixed scores or fixed answers.

Everyday features

  • Route of the day. Today → card "Today's loop": the coach picks one of the runner's own loops for today's outdoor run, with a map, the reason, "Open in Maps" and "Another loop". Needs a plan with an outdoor run today and loops under "My loops" (the same GPS route run at least twice in Apple Health). Not visible on an account without runs.
  • Fixed sessions. Training → the view switch "Week · Month · All sessions · Fixed sessions" → Fixed sessions (also the line under the week's plan). "+ Fixed session" adds one on one or more days with a time, a name and a kind; tap a session to edit, pause or remove it. Or write them in your own words (e.g. "parkrun Saturdays 9") or pick a photo of a schedule (read on the phone). In Tell your coach, "club on Tuesdays at 6" adds one too. The coach plans around them; days with one show a small pin in Week, plan rows a calendar-clock mark. Visible to a new account.
  • Together. The two-people button at the top right of every tab opens "Together": running buddies (their week's sessions, cheers, requests, invite, a code), running groups (members, the month's goal, create, join with a code), the rules, and report and block. A small orange dot means an unseen cheer or request. In Tell your coach, "invite Kim" makes an invite link. Visible to a new account.
  • The week in your calendar. More → Watches and apps → "Sessions in your calendar" → "Add the week's sessions automatically" (asks for calendar access). Mila writes the week's sessions to its own "Mila" calendar and reads back events you move or delete. Visible once the first plan exists; no Health data needed.
  • Today's session on the Lock Screen. A Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with "Done" and "Move to tomorrow" (Today then shows an undo card). Switch: More → Notifications → "Today's session on the Lock Screen" (on by default). It starts when the app is opened between 04:00 and 21:00 on a day with an unfinished session, so a new account sees it once the plan has a session today. On race day, for a race in the plan with a known start time, a countdown runs from 04:00 until 45 minutes after the start (rarely seen in review).
  • Run together nearby. With a Running buddy who shares their week, Today asks once per buddy "Do you and … live close enough to run together?" (Yes / No / Not now). The answer stays on the phone; no location is used. Each buddy then has "Suggest running together". Needs a second account paired as a buddy, with easy or long runs on the same day.
  • Race deadline reminders. Races → a race from Mila's race register shows registration closing, price steps and bib pickup, and asks "Have you registered?" (Yes / Not yet). Reminders come as notifications at 10:00 and open the race page; switch: More → Notifications → "Reminders about registration and bib pickup". Needs a race whose organiser dates the register already has.
  • Niggle follow-up. Write that something hurts (e.g. "my left calf is sore") in Tell your coach, after a run or in a note. If the coach judges it a niggle worth following, Today shows "Niggle: calf, day n" with Better / Same / Worse and a way to close it; the history is on Analysis. Visible to a new account by writing such a sentence.
  • Race matching. Races → Add race → Add manually: from three letters, up to three races from the register appear under "Did you mean …?". A race typed by hand that the daily job later finds in the register is linked with a note on the race ("Not the same race" undoes it). Type e.g. "Lidingo" to see it.
  • Shoe photo. Training → Around your running → Shoes → "Photograph the shoes" (also in "Import shoes"): take or choose up to six photos and the coach recognises brand and model; you confirm each pair before saving. Faces are blurred on the phone first. Visible to a new account.
  • Running form over time. Analysis → card "Running form over time": cadence, stride length, ground contact, vertical oscillation and power over 12 weeks, with a line from the coach. Shown only when runs in Apple Health have running dynamics (in practice an Apple Watch).
  • Treadmill today. Today → card "Treadmill today" when the coach judges the weather (storm, ice, heat, hard cold) or a trip makes running outside clearly worse: the session as treadmill steps (time, speed, incline), "Outdoors anyway" and, on a trip, nearby parks. Seen on a bad-weather day, or by telling the coach you are travelling.
  • Siri: "What am I running today?" An App Shortcut on iPhone and Apple Watch: say "What am I running today in Mila?" (Swedish: "Vad springer jag i dag i Mila?"). It answers from the app's own data with the coach's call and today's session; no new coach call. Works once the first plan exists.
  • Say how it felt (Apple Watch). After a run on the watch, under "How did it feel?", one dictated sentence goes to the coach as the run's note. Needs an Apple Watch and a run.
  • Dark out and Check In. Today → card "Dark out" when today's outdoor run starts before sunrise or ends after dark (from the calendar time, the usual start time, or now): "Choose contact" (the system picker) and "Check In with …", which opens a Messages draft where you add Apple's Check In yourself. Mila stores no location and sends nothing. Seen in the evening or early morning on a day with an outdoor run.
  • Share moments. A share button on a retired shoe ("Thanks for … km"), a milestone, a best effort, the VO₂max card, a group's month goal, the journey on Milestones, and a race page ("Shall we run this one?"). The pictures are made on the phone. Quickest: Training → Around your running → Shoes → add a shoe, retire it, share.
  • Dish pictures and taste memory. Every recipe and meal in "Food for the week" has a drawn picture of its kind of dish. Under a recipe: "Photograph it when it's done" (your photo replaces the picture, on this phone only) and later "Cook it again?". Training → Around your running → Food → "Taste memory" shows what the coach remembers about your taste, editable. Visible after asking for a recipe or a week's food.
  • Coach memory and Follow-ups. More → You and the coach → "What the coach remembers about you" (food, runs, races, strength, evenings, clothes; each editable and clearable). More → Settings → "Follow-ups": a switch per area. At most one follow-up question a day appears as a card on Today, never as a notification. A new account sees both pages (empty at first).
  • Goal assessment. When the coach reads your goal it also judges whether it is realistic for you: More → About you and your goal → "Your goal" → "The coach's assessment", with "Keep my goal" or "Switch to …" when it is not, and one line on Today until you choose. Try a goal like "a marathon next Friday, I have never run".
  • Consent version 6. The coach's consent screen ("I agree" / "Not now", with "What's sent?") now also names shoe and clothing photos, the taste memory, route summaries, free calendar times, the phone's travel hint, the registration answer and the coach's memory. A new account agrees to version 6 in onboarding. Someone who agreed to an older version is asked once, full screen; "Not now" keeps the earlier consent and turns off only these parts, and they can agree later under More → About you and your goal.

The first week

  • Apple Health history. Onboarding → "Your training data": a drawing of Apple's sheet shows "All Recorded Data and Future Data" before iOS asks (iOS 27). If the history Mila gets looks cut short, a note in the guide and a card on Today explain how to fix it in Settings ("Check again" / "That's right"). The note needs Health data that starts recently.
  • One question. Onboarding → "What do you want from your running?": one free-text question instead of a form. What the history already shows is listed under "What the coach already sees" (needs a few weeks of runs).
  • The coach's first look. Onboarding → "Your first plan": one line from the coach about your history ("The coach is looking at your history…" while it loads). Needs runs in Apple Health; without them nothing is shown.
  • Your first week. Today → card "Your first week", day n of 7, with three things to try ("Go for a run", "Say something to the coach", "See how your week fits together" or, with an Apple Watch, the watch in the morning) and "Hide". Visible right after onboarding. Near the end of the free week a card "Your first week with Mila" sums up the week and links to "Manage subscription".
  • Tips. Now and then the coach adds one sentence on Today about something you have not tried yet, with a button (e.g. "Photograph a meal", "Add a race") and "Not now"; at most one moment a day, never a notification. Switch: More → Follow-ups → "Tips from your coach". Today may also show one small ask such as "No watch? Record with your phone". The coach decides, so a new account may or may not see one.
  • Make a wish. More → Help and support → "Make a wish": write what you miss in Mila, optionally with a screenshot. The support answers and asks at most one question; the Mila team reads all wishes and replies in the same thread when one is done. Visible to a new account (needs the coach's consent).
  • Talk to your coach. Today → "Tell your coach" (also in Training and on the watch): the microphone button in the field turns speech into text on the device (on-device recognition only; no audio leaves the phone), which you can edit before sending. Replies can be read aloud ("Read the coach's replies aloud when I talk" under More → About you and your goal). Asks for microphone and speech recognition permission. Visible to a new account.
  • Food at the right time. The coach's food line on Today and its recipe offer ("Want me to suggest tonight's food?") follow the time of day and your usual meal times, so late in the evening it shows tomorrow's breakfast or nothing. Part of today's note; the coach writes it only when training makes food matter.
  • Course elevation. After a race from the register that you ran with GPS, the race page asks once "Would you like to help next year's runners with the course's elevation? Just the elevation profile, nothing about you." (Yes / Not now; setting under More → Watches and apps). Needs a real race and run.
  • Clothing advice in the morning. Today's weather box shows what to wear next to the best time window, also in the morning before today's note is written (from the line written the evening before). Needs an outdoor session and a day's history, so not on the first morning.
  • First run with Mila. After the first run, a one-time card on Today, "Your first run with Mila!", in the coach's words. Needs a real run.

Secrets (guideline 2.5.19)

Mila has seven small secrets to find. They are cosmetic only: an animation, a mark, a milestone or a friendly line in the coach's comment. They change no function, no plan and no data sent anywhere except the fact that a secret was found (stored with the account, deleted with it).

  • Documented in the app: More → Help → "Secrets" says that they exist and where they are listed, without saying how to find them.
  • Listed in the app: More → About Mila → "Secrets · n of 7", which appears once the first one is found. It shows each found secret with its day and the rest as "?".
  • All seven: the milestone "The Discoverer", and a one-time offer on Today of the alternate app icon "Gyllene Mila" (golden). The icon changes only when the user taps "Yes, please" or the switch "The golden icon" under More → About Mila → Secrets, through the system's alternate icon API, with the system's own confirmation.
  • How to find them: described in the Notes for Review in App Store Connect, so that runners can still discover them for themselves.

Server switches

Some server features have a switch so that they can be stopped quickly if something goes wrong. All of them are on in production, and none of them hides a screen:

  • PROOFREAD_ENABLED: a second pass that checks the coach's longer texts for language errors before they are stored. Runners never see it.
  • UGC_REVIEW_ENABLED: the automatic review of free text between runners (a cheer's own words, a chosen name, a group's name) before it is delivered; a harmful text is held, never delivered, and the sender is told calmly. Report and block stay as they are. Off, the word list alone decides.
  • GROUP_GOALS_ENABLED: the coach suggests a month's goal for a Running group (Together → Running group). Groups work the same without it, with a goal the admin sets.
  • RACE_DISCOVERY_ENABLED (off, paused): the daily job that adds new official races to Mila's race register. The races already in the register stay searchable under Races → Add race.
  • AI_CONSENT_ENFORCED and SUBSCRIPTION_ENFORCED: the server refuses coach calls without consent or an active subscription.

What needs real data

  • Runs in Apple Health: route of the day, running form over time, the first-week run feedback, Dark out (falls back to the current time), the elevation question, most follow-up questions, and most of the secrets.
  • An Apple Watch: "Say how it felt", running dynamics, race mode.
  • A second account: the "run together nearby" question (a Running buddy).
  • A race with known organiser dates or a race today: deadline reminders and the race-day countdown.
