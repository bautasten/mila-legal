Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to us with the form below and we'll get back to you. When you're signed in to the app, you can also reach support under More → Support.

Common questions

Does my watch work (Garmin, COROS, Suunto, Polar …)? Yes, if the watch's app syncs to Apple Health – Mila reads your runs from there. Turn it on in the watch's app (look for Apple Health or Health in its settings), then check under More → Watches and apps which apps Mila sees. Polar can also be connected directly there. Only Apple Watch has Mila's own watch app and can get the coach's sessions on the wrist; with other watches you follow the plan in the app.

How do I get Mila on my Apple Watch? The watch app comes with the iPhone app. If it doesn't appear on its own, open the Watch app on your iPhone, go to My Watch and install Mila under Available Apps. Add Mila's complication to a watch face or the Smart Stack. If the watch says "Open Mila on iPhone", open Mila on your phone once with the watch nearby.

Where are the coach's runs on my Apple Watch? In the Workout app, as their own row called Mila (not under Outdoor Run). Turn it on under More → Watches and apps → Sessions on Apple Watch. To send a single run, use Send to watch now; in Mila on the watch, Start opens today's session.

How do I use race mode? Add the race to your plan, from Mila's race directory or with a course file. Once the coach has made the forecast and pace plan, the race is on your watch from ten days before the start; tap Start race mode. The watch records the race without your phone, with pace and fueling cues, climb alerts and the course on the watch. Allow location on the watch, or no route is saved. The race is saved to Apple Health and reaches Mila like any other run.

How do I change today's session or the week? Tap Tell your coach on Today and describe in your own words how you feel or what's going on. The coach decides whether it concerns today's session or the rest of the week. The week can be re-planned twice a week, and the coach replies about today's session once a day. You can also move sessions in the plan yourself.

How do I find my official result? Turn on Fetch my results automatically (asked on a race's page, and found under More → About you and your goal → Official results), and Mila looks for your result after each race. The name you choose for results lists is kept only on your phone. You can also search yourself: open the race after the finish and tap Get my official result.

How do I film my running form? Under Training → Around your running → Running form. Film in the app, where the guide shows you how to place the phone, or pick videos from Photos. The video never leaves your phone: only still frames, cropped around you with every face blurred, are sent to the coach.

The coach writes no texts. The coach needs your consent before anything is sent to the external AI service. If you chose Not now, you can agree under More → About you and your goal → The coach and AI. The coach's texts are part of Mila Coach, which you can try free for a week.

The coach has no data about me. Allow Mila to read Apple Health (Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Mila → Turn On All). If iOS asks about history, choose All recorded data. You can also connect Polar under More → Watches and apps. Without data, the coach plans from your own description.

How do I cancel my subscription? In the Settings app on iPhone → your name → Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Under More → Subscription in the app you can see your status and restore purchases.

How do I delete my account? In the app: More → Account → Delete account. All data on your account is deleted from Mila's server and Sign in with Apple is withdrawn. A subscription is cancelled separately in Settings. If you connected Polar, remove Mila at account.polar.com yourself. A few records are kept, as described in the privacy policy.
