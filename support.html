Common questions

Does my watch work (Garmin, COROS, Suunto, Polar, Amazfit, WHOOP, Oura …)? Yes, if the watch's app syncs to Apple Health – Mila reads your runs from there. Turn it on in the watch's app (look for Apple Health or Health in its settings, e.g. Garmin Connect: Connected apps), then check under More → Watches and apps which apps Mila sees. Only Apple Watch can get the coach's sessions on the wrist; with other watches you follow the plan in the app.

Where are the coach's runs on my Apple Watch? In the Workout app, as their own row called Mila (not under Outdoor Run). Turn it on under More → Watches and apps.

How do I delete my account? In the app: More → Account → Delete account. All data on your account is deleted from Mila's server and Sign in with Apple is withdrawn. If you connected Polar, remove Mila at account.polar.com yourself. A few records are kept, as described in the privacy policy.

The coach has no data about me. Allow Mila to read Apple Health, or connect Polar under More → Watches and apps. Strava runs come in through Apple Health. Without data, the coach plans from your own description.
