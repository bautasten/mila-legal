Running buddy and Running group are for cheering each other on. The names you choose for buddies, group names and cheers must not contain harassment, threats, hate, sexual content or spam, and must not urge anyone to harm themselves. Mila has zero tolerance for such content and for those who send it. You can report a name, a group name, a cheer or a member to the Mila team and block the runner in the menu (…) next to it, and report one of the coach's answers with Report the answer (touch and hold the answer). The Mila team reviews every report within 24 hours. Content that breaks these rules is removed, and whoever sent it can be shut out of Running buddy and Running group, also with a new account.
